      How this was made

      • Which roles: the ads on this board on , so only the roles that pass bet’s title and location filters at the companies she follows. It describes that slice, not the whole German job market.
      • A role is one posting. Copies of the same ad for several cities count once.
      • Skills: Claude Haiku 5.5 read each ad and listed what it asks for as required or nice to have, each with a quote. A skill counts only if its quote appears word for word in the ad. Nobody has reviewed them by hand, and similar names are merged loosely (for example “crm systems” into “crm”).
      • Archetypes: each role was summarised without its company, embedded with Voyage, and clustered with a cap of three roles per company so big employers don’t dominate. The 21 groups and their descriptions are drafts. A role joins one only when it is clearly closest; otherwise it stays unassigned. “Broad” or “mixed” marks groups that still cover more than one kind of job.
      • Not live: recomputed by hand, last on . Job links may have closed since.
